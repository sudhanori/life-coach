"Life Coach" is a specialized knowledge-curation and advisement platform engineered for mid-to-senior business
leaders, entrepreneurs, and high-performance professionals. The platform synthesizes strategic insights, operational
frameworks, and leadership wisdom from globally recognized executives and thought leaders—such as Indra Nooyi
(corporate strategy and executive governance), Dr. Tara Swart (neuroscience, neuroplasticity, and cognitive
performance), and Emma Grede (entrepreneurship, brand execution, and operational agility).
Rather than relying on ungrounded AI impersonation, AdvisorLens operates on a strict Grounded Curator & Expert
Librarian Model. The platform ingests, indexes, and retrieves information from verified public artifacts—including
books, podcast interviews, keynote speeches, and articles. Every generated response provides exact inline citations
and deep-links directly to native commerce gateways (e.g., book purchases, masterclasses) and media timestamps
(e.g., Spotify and YouTube deep links).
